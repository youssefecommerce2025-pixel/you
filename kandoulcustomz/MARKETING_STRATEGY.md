## 📊 Phase 1 — Foundation (Month 1-2) — $0 to $200 Budget

### A. Organic Content Machine (TikTok & Instagram — ZERO COST)

The single most powerful move with a modest budget is **behind-the-scenes content**. This works because:
- The algorithm rewards authentic, niche content
- Custom apparel content has huge organic reach potential
- "Process videos" go viral regularly in this category

**Content Calendar (5-7 posts/week):**

| Content Type | Platform | Frequency | Expected Reach |
|-------------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| "How it's made" process videos | TikTok + Reels | 3x/week | HIGH — this niche gets 100K+ views organically |
| Before/After custom order reveals | TikTok + Reels | 2x/week | VERY HIGH — emotional, shareable |
| Customer unboxing reactions | All platforms | 1x/week | HIGH — social proof + emotional |
| "Je Suis Là" lifestyle posts | Instagram | Daily stories | Medium — brand building |
| Outfit-of-the-day featuring pieces | TikTok + Instagram | 2x/week | Medium |
| Educational (how DTG vs embroidery works) | TikTok | 1x/week | High (niche interest) |
| Price reveals + order walkthrough | TikTok | 1x/week | Very High (shopping content) |

**Most Viral TikTok Hooks to Use:**
- *"I made this for $X and sold it for $Y 🤯"*
- *"Someone sent me their idea and this is what I created 😭"*
- *"Rating custom t-shirt businesses as a custom t-shirt business"*
- *"The custom hoodie my customer cried over 👑"*
- *"How I make $XXX per order from my home studio"*
- *"Je Suis Là — here's what it means and why people won't stop buying it"*

### B. Email Capture Strategy
- Popup on website with 15% off offer (already integrated)
- Goal: 100 subscribers by end of Month 1
- Weekly email: 1 new product/story + 1 customer spotlight + 1 exclusive deal

### C. Google My Business
- Create a free Google My Business listing
- Add photos of completed orders
- Collect reviews from every customer
- **Free local SEO that drives buyers searching "custom t-shirts near me"**

## 📈 Phase 2 — Paid Acceleration (Month 3-4) — $200-$500 Budget

### Meta Ads Strategy (Facebook + Instagram)

**Your Pixel is already installed.** Use it to:
1. Build a Custom Audience from website visitors
2. Create Lookalike Audiences (1-3%) from buyers
3. Retarget cart abandoners (highest ROI ads possible)

**Campaign 1: Awareness — $5/day ($150/month)**
- **Objective:** Video views / Reach
- **Creative:** Best TikTok/Reel repurposed as ad
- **Audience:** US, 22-40, interests: streetwear, custom apparel, gifts, small business
- **Expected:** ~2,000-5,000 video views/day at this budget

**Campaign 2: Conversion — $3/day ($90/month)**
- **Objective:** Purchase / Add to Cart
- **Creative:** Before/After custom order reveal + "Shop now" CTA
- **Audience:** Retargeting — website visitors last 30 days
- **Expected ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** 3x-5x (retargeting converts much better)

**Budget Breakdown for $300/month:**
- Meta awareness ads: $150
- Meta retargeting: $90
- Content creation tools (Canva Pro or similar): $15
- Email marketing (Klaviyo free up to 250 contacts): $0
- **Total: ~$255/month**

### TikTok Ads Strategy ($100-$200/month)

**Campaign: Spark Ads (Boost organic posts that are already performing)**
- Take your best-performing organic TikTok videos
- Run them as "Spark Ads" (they look native, not like ads)
- Budget: $10-20/day for 7-10 days on top-performing videos
- **This is the #1 most cost-effective TikTok ad format for small brands**

**Targeting:**
- US only
- Age 18-38
- Interests: custom apparel, streetwear, gifts, DIY, personalization
- Behavior: frequent online shoppers

## 💡 Phase 3 — Scale & Referral (Month 5-6) — $500+ Budget

### Influencer / UGC Strategy (Budget-Friendly Version)

**Micro-influencer Outreach ($0-$200 per partnership):**

Don't go for celebrities. Target **micro-influencers with 5K-50K followers** in these niches:
- Streetwear / fashion (US-based)
- College students / Greek life (huge market for custom apparel)
- Small business owners / entrepreneurs
- Fitness communities (love custom matching sets)
- Sports teams / coaches

**How to approach them:**
- Send a FREE piece in exchange for a post/video
- Offer a 15% affiliate commission on sales they drive
- Tools: Creator.co, Collabstr (free to browse)

**Script for DMs:**
> *"Hey [Name]! I love your content. We make premium custom apparel at Kandoul Customz — Je Suis Là is our signature piece. I'd love to send you one on us, no strings attached. If you love it and want to share, we'll set up an affiliate link for you. Interested?"*

### Referral Program
- Offer existing customers $10 store credit for every referral that buys
- Every customer becomes a salesperson
- Cost: only when a sale happens (zero risk)
